Privacy Policy

BEYOND THE KNOWN is an independent digital knowledge platform covering science, space, artificial intelligence, blockchain, cryptocurrency and financial markets. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our Testnet preview website.

Information processed

The public website can be browsed without creating an account. We do not currently provide contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, analytics accounts, or payment processing on this website.

If you choose to use Pi Sign-In, Pi Network handles the authorization process. After you approve the requested username permission, the website receives a temporary Pi access token through the sign-in callback and sends it to our server endpoint for verification with the Pi Platform API. The website receives the Pi app-specific user identifier (UID) and username from that verification. The current preview keeps the UID and username in your browser's session storage for the current tab session. It does not create a persistent website account or store a user profile in our application database.

We do not ask for, collect, or need your Pi Wallet passphrase, private keys, or wallet recovery phrase. Never share them with us or anyone else.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. The hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request data and security logs to deliver, protect and maintain the service. We do not operate a separate analytics database. We cannot promise that infrastructure providers do not create or retain operational logs; please consult their privacy documentation for details.

Cookies and browser storage

The website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. During Pi Sign-In, session storage is used temporarily to validate the sign-in security state and, after successful verification, to keep the returned UID and username for the current browser tab session. Clearing browser site data or closing the session may remove this information.

Third-party services and links

Pi Sign-In relies on Pi Network services and the Pi Platform API. Our articles may link to Blogger and other external websites. Those services are operated by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

Testnet status and payments

This website is a Pi Testnet preview. Pi Sign-In is provided for identity-verification testing only. Memberships, paid access, Pi payments, wallet transactions and persistent user accounts are not currently enabled. Testnet activity should not be treated as a real-money transaction or a promise of future Mainnet functionality.

Children

The website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to this preview, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit sensitive credentials or financial secrets through this website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact method published on the BEYOND THE KNOWN blog.
